What is Sol

A conversation that remembers you.

Sol pays attention, remembers what you say and asks good questions, so you can work out what's next. It's built for Rider students, and for the next two weeks you get to tell us where it falls short.

What you submit

Three parts, one form.

Find the problems, suggest a fix, and show us what it looks like.

1 Weakness report

5+ issues you found

For each one, include:

  • What you tried
  • What happened
  • Why it matters to students
  • Severity: low, medium or high
  • A screenshot, if you can
2 Proposal

1–2 ideas to fix it

For each idea:

  • The student problem it solves
  • How it works
  • How we'd know it's working
3 Mockup or prototype

Show us what it looks like

A hand sketch, a Figma or Canva screen, or an AI-generated UI all count.

Code is welcome but not required. See examples ↓

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this legit?

Yes. The Sol Showdown is organized by the Brodsky Institute and supported by D.A.IS and FBLA. Codes came through your club or program, not from a random message, and you'll meet the Sol team in person on Nov 3.

Sol is not affiliated with Rider University. If you're unsure about a message, contact Lakshitha directly (details below).

Do I need to know how to code?

No. A hand sketch, a Canva screen or an AI-generated mockup all work. Code is welcome but never required.

Can I use AI tools?

Yes, for mockups. Just tell us which tool you used.

Can I enter as a team?

To be confirmed.

Who owns my ideas?

Covered in the participant agreement, which you'll see before you submit. Final wording coming soon.

Can I share what I see in Sol?

Sol is in a private pilot, so please don't post screenshots or details publicly. The participant agreement covers the specifics.

Can I come to the event without submitting?

Yes. Come for the food, the pitches and the costume raffle. Just register so we know how many people to plan for.

I'm not in a listed group. How do I get a code?

Email Lakshitha or use the request form and we'll send you one.
